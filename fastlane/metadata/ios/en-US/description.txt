Run, kitty, run! Race down one long corridor that winds in a square spiral to the heart of the labyrinth, where the goal room waits. Wolves roam every leg of the way. One touch and you're down.

PLAY TOGETHER
• Online lobbies for up to 8 kitties: create a lobby and share the code or invite link
• Cross-play: friends on iPhone, iPad, other phones and the web join the same lobbies
• Or run solo

NEVER LEAVE A KITTY BEHIND
When a kitty gets caught it leaves a revive circle. Run over it to bring your friend back. If every kitty is down, the wolves win the round.

THINGS TO KNOW
• Wolves get more numerous the closer you get to the middle, and the final stretch is the hardest
• Ice levels: put on your skates and slide, carve and dodge across the rink
• Checkpoint flags on the ice levels gather the team and revive everyone
• Pick-ups: speed boots, an extra life and a shield bubble
• Online hosts choose the mode: Run + Skate, Run only, or Skate only
• Finish a run to wear a crown in the next one

MADE FOR PHONES
A floating joystick appears wherever you put your thumb, so your finger never covers your kitty. Or tap a spot to run there.

FRIENDLY ONLINE PLAY
No accounts and no sign-in. Chat is filtered, can be hidden, and you can block or report any player.

No ads. No in-app purchases.
