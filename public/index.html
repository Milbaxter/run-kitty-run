Run Kitty Run

Run Kitty Run is a free online co-op game you play in your browser, for 1 to 32 players. You are a kitty in a labyrinth full of wolves. Reach the heart of the labyrinth, don't touch the wolves, and never leave a kitty behind: run over a fallen friend's circle to revive them.

Play solo, in local co-op, or online with friends on computers, phones and tablets. Run on foot by day and skate the ice by night through eight levels. Inspired by the classic Warcraft 3 custom map Run Kitty Run. No download and no account needed.
