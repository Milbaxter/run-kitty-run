🐾 Run Kitty Run

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Short version: Run Kitty Run needs no account (there is an optional one on the web), and has no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites. We collect basic play statistics on our own server. We never sell data. We use this information to run and improve the game and keep it safe.

This policy covers the Run Kitty Run game on the web and the Run Kitty Run apps for iOS and Android (together, "the game"), made by Run Kitty Run ("we").

What we process

Display name

When you play online you choose a display name (or get a kitty name like "Mochi"). It is shown to the other players in your lobby. It is kept in the server's memory only while you are connected, and is not stored afterwards unless it appears in a report (see below). You don't need to use your real name, and you shouldn't.

Chat messages

Lobby chat messages are relayed in real time to the players in the same lobby. They are filtered automatically for offensive words and links. The server keeps your last few messages in memory while you are connected so that they can be attached to a report if someone reports you; they are not written to disk otherwise and are gone when you disconnect.

Reports

If a player reports another player, we store the report: the reason, the lobby code, time, both players' display names and platform (web, iOS or Android), the reported player's recent chat messages and IP address. We use reports only to moderate the game (for example muting or blocking abusive players) and delete them when no longer needed, at the latest after 12 months.

Play statistics

The game sends visits, run starts, levels reached, run duration, deaths, rescues and wins to our server, together with the game mode, device category (desktop, touch, iOS or Android) and a random statistics ID stored on your device. The server keeps that ID with the first date it was seen and the IDs active on the current day, and saves aggregate play counts. It also receives level-generation mismatch reports to diagnose multiplayer problems. These statistics help us understand play and improve the game. They are not used for advertising or tracking across other apps or websites. Clearing your app or browser storage creates a new statistics ID; it does not delete IDs already stored on the server. The statistics ID is separate from the unlock progress ID.

Unlock progress

Multiplayer (online) games count your progress toward cosmetic unlocks (looks for your kitty, and a new song for the soundtrack). Without an active swag account, the server keeps that progress under a random ID that your browser makes and stores (no name, email or other details), together with which unlocked items you switched on. It is moved onto your swag account if you activate one, and deleted when your browser hasn't played Multiplayer for two months. Clearing your browser's site data loses the ID and with it the progress.

Invites and friends

So you can invite the people you played with, the server gives each player a friend ID: a one-way code made from the random unlock progress ID (it can't be turned back into it, and holds no name or other details). Players in your lobby receive your friend ID, and your browser keeps a list of the players you played Multiplayer games with (their display name, colour and friend ID, and which ones you starred as friends). That list stays on your device; the server keeps no friend lists. While you have the Multiplayer menu or a lobby open, players who have your friend ID can see whether you are online, in a lobby or in a game, and can invite you to their lobby (an invite shows their display name and lobby). You can block invites from a player, or choose in Settings who may invite you (everyone, friends only or nobody); those choices are stored on your device.

Legends board

When a team beats the final run, the legends board stores that win: the date, the game mode, the run time, each kitty's display name and colour, and the line each player chooses to sign it with (lines can be changed for 15 minutes after the win). Anyone can see the board in the game. It holds no account or contact details. To have an entry or your line removed, email us with the display name and the date.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the game, we store the text you write, your display name, game mode and level, and your browser/device type (user agent) so we can understand bugs. Please don't put personal information in feedback.

IP addresses and server logs

Like every internet service, our server sees your IP address when you connect. We use it to deliver the game, to rate-limit spam (for example how many feedback messages or reports can be sent) and in short-lived server logs for security and debugging. Logs are rotated and kept for a limited time.

On your device

The game stores a few settings in your browser or app storage (for example your sound and music settings, chosen name, muted players, the players you played with and your invite settings, and whether you accepted the online rules). These stay on your device and are never sent to us, except the random statistics ID and play events (see Play statistics), the random unlock progress ID (see Unlock progress) and, when you open the invite list, the friend IDs of the players on it, so the server can tell you which of them are online (see Invites and friends).

Optional account (web)

On the website you can choose to sign in with Google or Discord and activate a swag account by paying any amount (from €0.50): the total you've paid shows next to your kitty in online games, and the account keeps your game stats and the cosmetic items you unlock by playing Multiplayer games. If you sign in with Google, we store your Google account ID, your email address and first name (from Google); if you sign in with Discord, your Discord user ID and display name (no email). For either, we store the total you've paid and the IDs of your payments, your game stats (how often your team cleared each level in each mode, your fastest time on each, crowns and revives, from online games and as reported by your browser for solo and local games), your unlock progress (how often you've done each unlock challenge in each mode, counted by the game server) and which unlocked items you've switched on, whether your total is shown online, plus a sign-in token for each device. Other players only see your total and the unlocked items you've switched on (as looks on your kitty), never your name or email. Payments are handled by Stripe, who receive your payment details directly (we never see your card); see Stripe's privacy policy. Sign-in is handled by Google or Discord; see Google's privacy policy and Discord's privacy policy.

You can sign out or delete your account in the game (the Swag account button on the main menu). Deleting it hides your account right away and removes its data after 14 days (until then you can restore it by signing in again); a record of each payment (amount, date, email) is kept for our bookkeeping as the law requires. We process account data to provide the account you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and payment records to meet legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in or contact details are required to play.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone, except if required by law.
  • No in-app purchases in the apps.

Legal basis

Where the GDPR applies, we process this data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running a safe online game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), and to provide the game you asked to play (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Children

Run Kitty Run is made to be enjoyed by everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: accounts are optional, and online chat is filtered and can be hidden in the game ("Hide chat"). Players can block and report others. Parents who believe their child has shared personal information can contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. If you have no account, tell us your display name, the approximate time and the lobby code so we can find reports or board entries. For records saved under a random statistics or progress ID, we may need that ID to locate the record; we can help you find it. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update it on this page and change the date above.
