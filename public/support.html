🐾 Run Kitty Run

Support

Help for Run Kitty Run on the web, iPhone, iPad and Android.

How to play

  • Guide your kitty through the spiral maze to the goal in the middle without getting caught by the wolves.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD. Touch: drag anywhere on the screen to steer.
  • Grab items along the way: boots make you faster, shields protect you, extra lives keep you in the run.
  • Caught? A friend can revive you by running through your circle.
  • Multiplayer: create a lobby and share the 4-letter code or invite link, or join an open lobby. Up to 8 kitties per lobby.

FAQ

Can I play with friends on other devices?

Yes. Web, iOS and Android players all play on the same server and can join the same lobbies (cross-play). Just share the lobby code or invite link.

It says a new version is out / I can't connect online.

The online game was updated. Update the app from the App Store or Google Play (on the web, reload the page), then try again. Also check your internet connection.

Someone is being rude in chat.

Tap their name in the chat and choose Block or Report…. You can also turn chat off with Hide chat. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; players reported by several people are muted automatically.

Why did my name change?

Names that look offensive are replaced with a random kitty name. Pick a friendly one!

Do I need an account? Does it cost anything?

No. The game is free to play with no ads, and you never need an account. Optional: in the iOS app, swag packs (in-app purchases through Apple) add to your swag number next to your kitty online and activate a swag account that keeps your stats and unlocks; on the website the same account works with Google or Discord sign-in. See the Terms and Privacy Policy.

I reinstalled the app or got a new iPhone. Where is my swag?

Open Swag account and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Your swag account goes with that Apple Account. Refunds for packs go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The game is slow or laggy.

Close other apps and tabs, and play on a stable Wi-Fi connection.
