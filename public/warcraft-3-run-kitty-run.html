Run Kitty Run and the Warcraft 3 custom map

Run Kitty Run started life as a Warcraft 3 custom map. This site is a new, free browser game made as a tribute to it. Here is how the two relate, and what's different.

▶ PLAY IN YOUR BROWSER No Warcraft 3 needed · free · 1 to 32 players

The map you remember

If you played Warcraft 3 custom games, you probably know it. Everyone in the lobby gets a kitty. The map is a long winding path full of wolves, and your team has to get to the end of it. Touch a wolf and you're out, until a teammate runs over the spot where you fell and brings you back. Then somebody dies two steps from the finish and the whole lobby groans.

It was simple enough to explain in one sentence and hard enough that plenty of lobbies never finished it, and it has been remade and re-versioned by the community for years. Many players remember it by its soundtrack, since popular versions played a Linkin Park song on loop.

Is the Warcraft 3 map still played?

Yes. It still shows up in the custom games list of Warcraft 3: Reforged, and remastered versions of the map are shared on community sites such as Hive Workshop. How easy it is to find a full lobby depends on your region and the time of day.

That is one of the reasons this game exists. Here you don't need to own Warcraft 3, install anything or wait for a lobby to fill. You open a link and you're a kitty.

What we kept

  • Kitties and wolves. One touch and you're down.
  • Revive circles. A fallen kitty leaves a circle, and a teammate running through it brings the kitty back.
  • The long way in. One winding path to the middle, with safe spots at the corners to catch your breath.
  • The lobby. A crowd of kitties that only gets there by going back for each other.

It is a new game, not a port. Nothing in it comes from Warcraft 3: the 3D kitties and wolves, the square-spiral labyrinth, the ice skating, the items, the music and the final run were all made for this game. The how to play page has the full rules.

Playing it like the old days

  1. Open runkittyrun.fun and choose Multiplayer.
  2. Create a lobby, or join one of the open ones.
  3. Paste the invite link into your Discord or group chat.
  4. Start the run, and go back for the kitty that died by the first corner.
